Pleep connects to Instagram through Meta's official Instagram API (the Instagram Graph API with Facebook Login for Business). You never enter your Instagram password in Pleep: you sign in with your Facebook profile, select your Page and Instagram account, and approve the permissions. After that, your AI agent answers Instagram Direct messages, and the settings unlock [comment to DM replies](/en/integrations/instagram/comment-to-dm).

Connecting takes a few minutes. We recommend doing it on a computer.

## Requirements

- **An Instagram professional account**: Business or Creator. Personal accounts do not work.
- **Your company's Facebook Page**, linked to that Instagram account.
- **A personal Facebook profile** that can manage that Page. You sign in with it in the Facebook window.
- **Message access turned on in Instagram** (step 1 below).
- **A free channel slot on your plan.** If you have reached the channel limit, Pleep offers to upgrade.

## Permissions Pleep requests

In the Facebook window you approve access to your account. Instagram Direct messaging needs these permissions:

- `instagram_basic`
- `instagram_manage_messages`
- `pages_messaging`
- `pages_manage_metadata`
- `pages_show_list`

Comment replies additionally need `instagram_manage_comments`. If your account was connected before comment replies existed, Pleep shows a "New: comment replies" banner with a **Reconnect** button.

## Step-by-step

<Steps>

### Step 1. Turn on message access in Instagram

In the Instagram app, open **Settings → Messages and story replies → Connected tools → Allow access to messages** and turn the toggle on.

Menu names differ between app versions. In some versions the toggle sits under **Message requests**.

<Callout type="warn" title="Without this setting the agent sees no messages">
  Instagram accepts the connection but never sends your customers' messages to Pleep. The setting is per Instagram account, so turn it on for every account you connect.
</Callout>

### Step 2. Open Instagram in Pleep

1. Sign in to Pleep and select your AI agent.
2. In the side menu, open **Integrations**.
3. Select **Instagram**.
4. Tick **"I turned on message access in Instagram"**. The sign-in button stays disabled until you do.
5. Click **Login**.

### Step 3. Complete the Facebook window

1. Click **Continue as** followed by your name. If it is the wrong profile, choose **Log in to another account**.
2. If a business selection appears, select the business that owns the Page and the Instagram account, then click **Continue**.
3. On **Choose the Pages…**, select **Opt in to current Pages only**, tick the Page linked to your Instagram account, and click **Continue**.
4. On **Choose the Instagram accounts…**, select **Opt in to current Instagram accounts only**, tick the correct @username, and click **Continue**.
5. On **Review… access request**, check the selected accounts and click **Save**. Click **Got it** if it is shown.

### Step 4. Finish in Pleep

If you granted access to several accounts, Pleep shows **"Choose an Instagram account"**. Tick the accounts you want and click **Connect**.

When the connection succeeds, you see **"Bot successfully created"** and the account appears under **Connected accounts**.

### Step 5. Check that it works

1. From a different Instagram account, send a new Direct message to the connected account.
2. Open **Chats** in Pleep and check that the message is there.
3. Check that the AI agent replied. Automatic replies need the AI to be turned on for that conversation.

</Steps>

## Settings after connecting

- **Account switch** ("Integration enabled" / "Integration disabled"). While it is off, the agent does not process messages from this account and does not answer comments.
- **Typing effect.** The customer sees "typing…" while the agent prepares a reply.
- **Connect another account.** One agent can have several Instagram accounts within your plan. Broadcasts and scheduled messages always send from the primary account.
- **Comments to DMs.** See [How to send a private reply to Instagram comments](/en/integrations/instagram/comment-to-dm).

## Common errors

### "No Instagram business account found"

Pleep did not find a professional Instagram account in your selection. Check that the account is Business or Creator and is linked to the Facebook Page you ticked. On your next sign-in, click **Edit previous settings** to change the selection.

### "All available Instagram accounts are already connected"

Every account you granted access to is already connected. To add another one, click **Edit previous settings** when signing in and tick it.

### Messages do not show up in Pleep

- Check message access in Instagram (step 1).
- Check that the correct @username is connected.
- Check that the account switch is on.

### "Instagram got disconnected" banner

Instagram revoked access, so the agent is not answering messages. Click **Reconnect**. Conversations and settings are preserved when you reconnect.

### "You signed in to a different Instagram account"

You picked the wrong account while reconnecting. Sign in again and choose the account that is connected in Pleep.
